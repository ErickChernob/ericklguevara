Stock that
Rocks


E-Commerce


Stock that Rocks is a start up and e-commerce website intended for the sale of digital stock photography in the US market, established in Phoenix, Arizona; its photographic and UI design style is clean and minimalist.

Client
Asociacion de
Industriales Mexicanos
Role
Web Designer
Year
2020 - 2022


Stock that Rocks offers a robust platform with the ability to grant free accounts to its users, in which they can create albums with their favorite photos and then proceed with their purchase. For its contributors, it offers special accounts where they can create their profile, upload their own material and, after an approval process, put it up for sale within the platform to generate income. After 2 years of successful operations the company got acquired and merged to another stock photography website.
