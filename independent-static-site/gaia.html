Gaia


Streaming


Gaia is an alternative media streaming platform established in Colorado. It offers the largest online resource of consciousness-expanding videos, over 8,000 informative and enlightening films, original shows, yoga and meditation classes.

Client
Gaia / Wizeline
Role
Web Designer
Year
2022


Through a collaborative process, we generated a set of landing pages mainly focused on the Yoga on Gaia international campaign where those interested sign up to gain access to a series of available classes and documentaries. Considering the target audience and their interests, we focused on creating a dynamic, fresh and colorful site, with language variants in french, spanish and german.
